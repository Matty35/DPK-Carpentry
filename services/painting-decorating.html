Expert Craftsmanship

Decoration That Transforms Any Space

Dan at DPK Carpentry & Decorating is as much an expert painter and decorator as he is a carpenter — with nearly two decades of experience delivering exceptional decorating results for residential and commercial clients across High Wycombe, Beaconsfield, Gerrards Cross, Marlow, Windsor, Henley-on-Thames, The Chalfonts and Central London.

Quality decorating is about far more than applying paint. It is about meticulous surface preparation — filling, sanding, priming and sealing — so that the finished coat looks and lasts as it should. We work with premium trade-specification paints and materials from leading manufacturers, and bring the same attention to detail to a single room refresh as to a complete commercial warehouse refurbishment.

Because Dan carries out both the carpentry and the decorating, you get a seamless result — new joinery and panelling painted to the same standard as the surrounding walls, with no gaps between trades and no handover issues. The Full Service

What's Included

►

Interior Painting & Decorating

Complete interior decoration for walls, ceilings, woodwork and joinery. All paint types including emulsion, eggshell, satinwood and gloss. Filling and repair of damaged plasterwork included.

►

Exterior Painting

Preparation and painting of render, timber cladding, fascias, soffits, guttering, windows and doors using high-quality weatherproof coatings designed to withstand the elements and protect your property.

►

Wall Panelling & Wainscoting

Bespoke wall panelling, dado rails, picture rails and wainscoting fitted and decorated to an exceptional standard — a popular choice for period homes and high-specification refurbishments across the area.

►

Wallpaper Hanging & Removal

Professional wallpaper hanging for all types including feature walls, full rooms, delicate hand-printed papers and heavy commercial vinyls. Thorough preparation ensures a perfectly smooth result every time.

►

Spray Painting

Professional spray finishing for kitchen cabinets, furniture, built-in joinery and other woodwork — delivering a factory-quality finish far superior to brush or roller application. A cost-effective alternative to full replacement.

►

Dustless Sanding

Our advanced dustless sanding system creates a flawless surface for painting while capturing 99% of dust at source — ideal for occupied homes and commercial premises where a clean environment matters.

►

Coving & Cornice Painting

Coving and cornice installation, repair and painting — carried out with the precision that period properties in Henley-on-Thames, Beaconsfield and Marlow demand.

►

Commercial & Industrial Decorating

Large-scale commercial projects including warehouse refurbishments, office fit-outs and retail spaces. Steel painting, industrial coatings, anti-slip floor coatings and fire-rated paint systems. Out-of-hours working available.

►

Feature Walls & Decorative Finishes

Feature walls, colour consultancy, specialist paint finishes and decorative effects to give your rooms character and impact. We advise on colour and finish to suit the light, proportions and style of your home.

How It Works

Our Process

1

Free Consultation

We visit your property, assess the surfaces and discuss your requirements, colour choices and timeline.

2

Preparation

All surfaces are properly filled, sanded and primed. Preparation is the most important stage — it is where a lasting finish begins.

3

Expert Decoration

Decoration is carried out using premium materials and the right technique for each surface — brush, roller or spray as appropriate.

4

Clean Handover

We protect your furniture and floors throughout, clean up completely on completion, and leave only when you are fully satisfied.
