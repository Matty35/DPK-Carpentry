Fire Door Installation Done Correctly

A fire door is only as effective as its installation. At DPK Carpentry & Decorating, we specialise in fire door installation across Buckinghamshire for both residential and commercial clients — bringing the same precision to every door that safety-critical work demands. FAQ

Common Questions

FD30 doors provide 30 minutes of fire resistance and are standard for most residential properties. FD60 doors provide 60 minutes of resistance and are typically required in commercial settings or higher-risk areas.

Fire doors are a legal requirement in certain situations, including HMOs, flats and commercial buildings. Building regulations and fire safety law set out where they must be installed. We can advise on compliance during your free consultation.

Costs vary depending on door specification, number of doors and the complexity of the installation. We provide free written quotes for all fire door projects.

A single fire door typically takes two to four hours to install correctly including all hardware and intumescent seals. We give a clear timeline at the quote stage.
