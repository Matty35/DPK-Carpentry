Outdoor Spaces Crafted With Precision

A well-designed deck transforms a garden into a true extension of the home — and for homeowners seeking decking installation in Buckinghamshire, the quality of the build is everything. At DPK Carpentry & Decorating, we design and construct outdoor spaces that are as carefully considered as any interior project. From garden decking in High Wycombe built around mature planting to bespoke decking in Marlow engineered to make the most of a riverside aspect, every project is planned meticulously before a single post is set.

We work with both premium hardwood and softwood timbers and market-leading composite boards to suit every taste and budget. Our composite decking in Beaconsfield projects are particularly popular with homeowners who want a beautiful terrace with virtually no ongoing maintenance. For those who prefer the warmth of natural wood, our timber decking in Henley-on-Thames work showcases what properly selected, correctly treated hardwood can achieve in a garden setting.

FAQ

Common Questions

Decking costs depend on size, material choice and ground conditions. We provide free written quotes for all decking projects. Call 07766 314197.

Timber decking has a natural look and lower upfront cost. Composite decking requires almost no maintenance and lasts significantly longer. We advise on the right choice for your garden and budget.

In most cases domestic decking does not require planning permission, but there are exceptions for raised decks or listed buildings. We advise on this as part of our consultation.

A well-built composite deck can last 25 years or more with minimal maintenance. Hardwood timber decks last 15 to 20 years with regular treatment.
