Expert Craftsmanship

Joinery That Transforms Your Home

When it comes to finding a trusted carpenter in Buckinghamshire, the difference between a good result and an exceptional one lies in both skill and materials. At DPK Carpentry & Decorating, Dan brings 19 years of hands-on experience to every project — from fitted wardrobes in High Wycombe that maximise every inch of space, to bespoke wardrobes in Beaconsfield crafted to complement period properties with exacting precision.

Our alcove shelving in Gerrards Cross has become a hallmark of our work: clean lines, solid construction and a finish that is indistinguishable from the finest bespoke joinery. Whether you need new joinery in Marlow, crisp new skirting boards in Windsor, or a complete second-fix carpentry package throughout your home, every detail is planned, prepared and executed to the highest standard.

We work exclusively with quality-graded timber and premium fixings, and take the time to properly understand your space before a single measurement is taken. The result is carpentry that looks beautiful on day one and remains so for years to come — furniture-quality joinery at a fair, transparent price, with a City & Guilds qualified craftsman overseeing every stage.
