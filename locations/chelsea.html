Chelsea has long been one of London's most celebrated addresses, and the standard of workmanship that SW3 and SW10 clients expect reflects that status entirely. From the elegant Victorian townhouses on tree-lined streets to the sought-after garden flats and mews cottages that characterise so much of Chelsea, every property here commands a level of craft and attention to detail that leaves no room for compromise. DPK Carpentry & Decorating works regularly in Chelsea, often alongside interior designers and architects overseeing significant renovation projects, and our reputation in the area is built on consistently delivering that exceptional standard. Bespoke fitted joinery is at the heart of much of our Chelsea work — wardrobes designed and built to integrate seamlessly with period rooms, alcove shelving with proportions that respect the original architecture, and panelling and other bespoke elements that elevate the character of a space. Flooring installation across Chelsea properties draws on our full range: wide-plank engineered oak, solid hardwood, premium LVT laid with precision across open-plan reception floors and hallways. Interior decorating in Chelsea demands thorough surface preparation, considered colour advice and paint systems that achieve both the depth of finish and the durability that busy London homes require.
