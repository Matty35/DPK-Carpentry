Chalfont St Giles is one of the most beautiful villages in the Chilterns — a place where the historic village centre, with its flint-and-brick cottages, duck pond and the famous Milton's Cottage, sits within the protected landscape of the Chilterns Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The HP8 postcode encompasses a varied mix of properties, from the period and listed buildings at the heart of the conservation area to larger detached family homes on the roads that lead out towards the surrounding woods and fields. DPK Carpentry & Decorating has worked throughout Chalfont St Giles for many years and understands the particular demands of working in and around period properties in a conservation area. In the older properties, this means joinery work sympathetic to the character of the building — matching period mouldings, working around uneven surfaces, selecting materials and finishes that complement the existing fabric of a cottage or farmhouse. For the newer and larger homes in and around Chalfont St Giles, our clients come to us for fitted wardrobes in Chalfont built to exact measurements, for high-quality flooring installation, for kitchen fitting and for interior decoration of the quality that these homes deserve. Across the HP8 area and into neighbouring villages such as Little Chalfont, Amersham and Gerrards Cross, DPK is a trusted name in carpentry and decorating.
